# Lunar books, manuals, and guides: inventory, ingest, and lore

Researched 2026-10-09. Scans and OCR text are local only, in `lunar_scripts/books/`
(see its README); `tools/lunar_scripts/books.py` fetches and OCRs them. Canon rule
applied throughout: only Lunar 1 and Lunar 2 are canon, Japanese first; everything
else is judged by fidelity to them (consistent, extra flavor, or contradiction).

## Summary

- **The "three races crossed, a fourth fell" story is not in any Working Designs
  print I could find.** I checked both Sega CD manuals, the 1994 Silver Star guide, both
  PS1 hardcover manuals, and both PS1 strategy guides, and none has it. No Japanese source
  backs it either. The Eternal Blue Sega CD manual, the book English Wikipedia cites, says
  only: "Many believe that the inhabitants of LUNAR lived on the Blue Star before the
  Goddess brought them to this world." Treat the race list as an uncited wiki invention.
- **No print source names a first Dragonmaster, "Louie" or otherwise.** It is the same
  across all 21 ingested items, Japanese and English.
- **The best Japanese find is already scanned on the Internet Archive:** *LUNAR Ⅰ・Ⅱ
  公式設定資料集* (SoftBank, 1995). It holds "LUNARに関する30の質問", answered by
  scenario writer 重馬敬 (Kei Shigema), plus his early drafts and a long interview with
  him and Kubooka.
  - It says Lunar began as an emergency refuge from the collapsed, frozen Blue Star, and
    that Althena *and the dragons* remade it into the magic world.
  - It says the dragons are not animals but crystallized Lunar magic.
  - It says Althena, Lucia, and Zophar are probably the same kind of being.
  - It says Lucia does not reincarnate the way Althena does.
  - It says beastmen (獣人族) come in several kinds.
  - It records Kubooka designing Lucia's medallion as the crest of whoever holds "Althena's
    role, generation after generation" (代々アルテナの役目).
- **Purchase-only items most likely to carry more lore:** Tokuma's 1992 *ルナの世界と攻略ガイド*
  (a "history of Lunar and Althena" section and an original side story), Shigema's four
  Silver Star Story novels, the three-volume *青き星のルーシア* novels, and the manga
  *ヴェーン飛空船物語*. All are cheap used; the shopping list is at the end.

## 2. What was ingested, and how well it OCRs

All 21 items were fetched from the Internet Archive, except the Eternal Blue Sega CD
manual, which came from gamesdatabase.org. They were OCRed with Tesseract 5.4: `jpn+eng`
with tessdata_best models, or `eng`. A post-pass removes the spaces Tesseract puts
between Japanese characters. Page counts and per-item notes are in
`lunar_scripts/books/README.md`.

- **Typeset English (the Working Designs guides and PS1 hardcovers):** good.
  Sidebars over artwork come out partial.
- **The Eternal Blue Sega CD manual:** poor, because its script font sits over
  illustrations. I read its story pages by eye.
- **Japanese body text on plain backgrounds:** usable after the space pass. This covers
  the art book Q&A and interviews, the World Guide, and the Saturn and MPEG manuals.
  Expect some wrong kanji and stray furigana.
- **Japanese text over art, captions, and handwriting:** poor to unusable. This covers
  the Mega-CD manuals' story pages, the Saturn L2 prologue, and the art book's sketches.
- **The Harmony Prima guide:** exact, because the PDF has a text layer.
- **The Harmony manual:** uses the uploader's poor OCR layer.

Every lore passage quoted below was checked against the page image, not the OCR. Page
numbers are the printed page; `pdf N` is the page in the scan.

## 3. Lore passages and judgments

### 3.1 Creation of Lunar, Althena, and the Blue Star

**JP canon baseline.** The Saturn Lunar 2 prologue says Lunar 「かつて空気すらない死の大地」
was turned green by 「創造の女神アルテナ」. Lucia's Goddess Tower history (O064) says the
war with Zophar wounded the Blue Star and left it uninhabitable. 「かろうじてゾファーを封印」:
after sealing Zophar, Althena 「死の星であったルナを緑あふれる大地へと変え、生き残ったわずかな
人々とともに移り住んだ」 (`lunar_scripts/l2eb_saturn_jp/prologue_jp.txt`,
`goddess_tower_jp.txt`).

| Source | Passage | Judgment |
|---|---|---|
| 設定資料集 Q1, p.109 (pdf 111), Shigema | 「崩壊し、永遠の冬を迎えた青き星からの緊急避難場所というのが、最初のLUNARの役割」; then 「アルテナの女神と竜たちによって、新しい文明の創造と、青き星の再生のために『魔法世界LUNAR』として、造り変えられた」 | **Consistent, plus extra flavor.** It adds two things the games never say. The Blue Star died into an eternal winter, matching the snow on the Blue Star in the L2 opening. And the dragons helped Althena reshape Lunar, so dragons are as old as the settlement. |
| 設定資料集 Q2, p.109, Shigema | Lunar is 「青き星再生のための、いわばエネルギー発生、放射装置」: the magic of everyone living on Lunar is used to make the frozen Blue Star green again. 「長い年月の後に、その記憶は失われ、青き星は伝説の故郷としてのみ人々の心に残った」 | **Consistent** with Lucia's in-game mission and with JP Lunar 1 treating the crossing as 伝説. |
| 設定資料集 Q3, p.109, Shigema | 「アルテナ、ルーシア、そしてゾファーは、おそらく存在としては同じもの」; he declines to say what they are (「SFではないので、あいまいなままで」) | **Extra flavor**, no contradiction. It is useful if the rewrite needs Althena's nature: a god-class being of the same kind as her enemy. |
| 設定資料集 Q4, p.109 | For the dying world, Althena and Lucia were 「全人類の最後の希望」, and so bear responsibility for everything that was meant to perish then | **Extra flavor**; it fits the penance reading in `story-bible.md`. |
| Mega-CD and Saturn Lunar 2 manual prologue, p.2 (both versions) | 「創造の(女)神アルテナ。竜を駆るドラゴンマスター。空飛ぶ都。世界を救った英雄たち。そして、天空にかがやく青き星。」 | **Canon** (the manual of the canon game); only lists legends. |
| Saturn SSS manual, p.2 「ルナの世界」 (same text in the 1997 MPEG manual and both Lunar Legend books) | Lunar is 「天に青き星の輝く神秘の世界」; magic is everyday because 「大地や大気に満ちている女神アルテナの魔法力」 is its source | **Canon-adjacent**; no creation account. |
| Lunar Legend manual, p.4 (GBA, 2002) | A creation poem: a 「一筋の光」 descends from the Blue Star; green is born on a dead land; 「女神アルテナ / この星『ルナ』に命の息吹を与え人々を導いた優しき女神」; and 「いつしか人々はアルテナを神として崇め祈りを捧げるようになった」 | **Consistent, plus extra flavor.** The interesting line is that worship came later ("in time, people came to revere Althena as a god"), which suits a goddess who never asked to be one. |
| WD Sega CD Eternal Blue manual, p.4 | "Many believe that the inhabitants of LUNAR lived on the Blue Star before the Goddess brought them to this world." It also says "Over a thousand years have passed since Dragonmaster Alex..." | **Consistent.** This is the whole of what the manual Wikipedia cites says about the founding. |
| WD L2EBC opening narration (WD L2EBC guide, p.92, pdf 94) | "Time and circumstance conspired to force Althena to populate Lunar with people from the Blue Star... Thousands of years passed, burying the origin and purpose of the Blue Star" | **Extra.** "Thousands of years" is not in the Japanese Saturn prologue (はるかな昔). |
| WD L2EBC "Death of the Blue Star" (WD L2EBC guide, pp.241-242) | "In the end, the Blue Star was laid to waste... as Zophar intended. However, all hope was not lost." | **Divergence.** "As Zophar intended" is a Working Designs reading; the Japanese line says only that the Blue Star was wounded and uninhabitable. Use the Japanese line. |
| 設定資料集, Shigema's rejected 1992-11 draft, p.83 (pdf 85) | Lunar's core holds a creation device, 「ジェネシス」, that turned the dead moon into a living world. Only Althena can trigger it, and it was used once. 「地球には予備の女神が眠っている。それがルーシア」. The draft also has a vision of a war 「千年前」 near the magic city Althena, where a machine-castle fires on a mysterious star and Vane breaks away from Althena. | **Discarded draft, not canon.** It is flavor at most. It is worth knowing that "Genesis" was Shigema's word for the creation device a decade before Dragon Song was titled ルナ ジェネシス in Japan. |

### 3.5 Althena's rebirths

| Source | Passage | Judgment |
|---|---|---|
| JP canon (O065, Luna's recording) | 「私は絶対の神として この世界で転生を繰り返しながら人々を導き」, until 「最後の転生」 to live as one human | Canon baseline |
| 設定資料集 Q29, p.111 | Asked whether Lucia also reincarnates 「アルテナのように」: 「しません」. She alone went to sleep on the Blue Star to wait for its rebirth | **Consistent**; it confirms Althena's rebirth cycle as the writer's intent. |
| 設定資料集, Shigema and Kubooka interview, p.100 (pdf 102) | Kubooka: 「ルーシアのこのメダリオンっていうのは、一応代々アルテナの役目をするためのひとつの紋章のつもりで考えてたもの」 | **Extra flavor**, no contradiction. "Althena's role, held generation after generation" frames godhood as an office passed between incarnations. That fits the penance reading (rebirths as terms served) and suggests a physical token Dragon Song's Althena could carry. |
| 設定資料集 interview, p.100 | Shigema: Lucia was first conceived as 「中立の忠誠なる審判官」 who weighs humanity and destroys it if the scale tips. Kubooka: there was also a 「創造と破壊のアルテナ」 phase | Discarded concepts; flavor only. |
| 設定資料集 interview, p.99 | Shigema cut hero-descendant and bloodline material from Lunar 2 on purpose: 「英雄の子孫で遺伝子がどうとか、血統がどうのというのはやっぱりいらない」 | **Writing guidance** from the series' writer. It argues against making Jian or Ignatius matter by lineage. |

## 4. Shopping list for Jeff, in priority order

1. **Tokuma わんぱっく ルナ ザ・シルバースター ルナの世界と攻略ガイド** (1992). It is the
   only book known to have a "history of Lunar and Althena" section, plus an original
   side story, all from the canon game's own year. About ¥1,100 ($7) on Yahoo! Auctions;
   check whether you are getting わんぱっく 137 or 138, because the database mixes them.
2. **重馬敬, LUNAR シルバースターストーリー novels 1-4** (Sneaker, 1996-2000). The series'
   own scenario writer, at length. About ¥400-700 each, or ¥2,100 for vols 1-3 as a set.
   Vol. 4 is the scarcest.
3. **LUNAR2 エターナルブルー 青き星のルーシア 1-3** (細江ひろみ from Shigema, 1998). These
   are the most likely to cover the Blue Star, Zophar, and Althena in prose. About ¥495
   each.
4. **LUNAR ヴェーン飛空船物語** (船戸明里, 1998; reissue 2009). Vane's past with the 魔族,
   plus young Ghaleon and Dyne. ¥220-1,000 used, or buy the ebookjapan edition today.
5. **ルナ-ジェネシス-公式コンプリートガイド** (SoftBank, 2005, 4-7973-3231-X). The only
   Japanese book on Dragon Song; get it for any setting notes on its era. No recent price
   found.
6. **ルナ シルバースターストーリー ニュータイプ100%コレクション** (Kadokawa, 1997). Cast
   profiles and location art from the remake. No recent price found.
7. **Prelude and Lunatic Parade drama CDs** (TYCY-5393, 5444, 5452). The Shigema-era
   story extras; about ¥1,700 each. Low text value without transcription.
8. Optional: *LUNAR 幼年期の終り* (¥500), the *Lemina* novel (¥550), BEEP! World Guide
   Vol.2, and the 電撃 Lunar 2 guide's "world" section (¥790-1,700).

The 1995 設定資料集 is already ingested from a scan; buy it (about ¥3,500) only if you
want the book. Unofficial English scans of four manga sit on the Internet Archive's
manga collection. I did not fetch them; whether to use them is your call.
