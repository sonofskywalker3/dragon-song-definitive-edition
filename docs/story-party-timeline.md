## Playable cast

Five playable characters: Jian, Lucia, Gabryel, Flora, Rufus.

- **Confirmed** no sixth playable or guest character appears in the LP (read in full), Bolner, Retro, Ripclaw or darklao.
  darklao's Specials list covers only these five (Althena Magic for Lucia/Flora, Black Magic for Jian, Beast Magic for
  Gabryel/Rufus). DCblog: "Her [Flora] and Rufus basically round out the playable cast." Hoard reads the five people on
  the box art as the five playable characters.
- **Confirmed** the battle party never has more than 3 members, and the vanilla game never gives the player a choice
  of who fights. Every stretch below has at most 3 characters available, and all changes are forced by story.
- Solo stretches (Jian alone) and a two-person stretch exist; see the timeline.

Characters ever playable at the same time as each other: Jian is always there; Gabryel is there from Healriz to the end
except steps 6, 7 and 12 to 13. Lucia never overlaps Flora or Rufus. Rufus never overlaps Lucia or Flora. So with free
swapping, a fourth or fifth character only becomes possible if the hack brings back Lucia and/or Rufus (story-dead
or kidnapped) or keeps Flora during step 12 to 18.

## Leveling

- **Confirmed** no source mentions any player choice on level up. Spells arrive automatically by level, and the game
  does not even announce them: LP U01 ("I'm not sure if she got it on level 2 or level 3 because the game does not
  tell you"). Lucia learns her last spell around level 8 (LP U03 "now Lucia knows all the spells she'll ever get").
  Flora has the same 8-spell list and had all of them by level 10 (LP U08). Gabryel only ever has Gale Cut (LP U04),
  Rufus only Thunder Sword (LP U08), both 10 MP. Jian's 4 Black Magic spells come from the 4 Dragon Rings (story),
  one ring equipped at a time in his single accessory slot (LP U11, U12). darklao lists all specials.
- **Confirmed** one shared experience (Althena Conduct) table for everyone (darklao, "AC Cost of Levels"), gained only
  in Virtue Mode (Retro, Ripclaw).
- **Confirmed** enemy stats, boss HP and AC rewards scale with level; LP U04 and Ripclaw say it follows Jian's level
  specifically. Because AC rewards scale too, a level 1 newcomer catches up fast (LP U04, U08; Hoard).
- **Uncertain** whether a party member not in the battle party gains AC (never arises in vanilla since nobody is
  benched).

## Do returning characters keep their level and gear?

Only Flora (step 12 to 19) and Gabryel (steps 6 to 8 and 12 to 14) ever return.

- **Gabryel's level**: **confirmed** not reset. Bolner's levels at the Elda Canyon boss (Jian 24, Gabryel 23) show she
  returns at her own level. **Gear**: no source warns to strip her and none reports losing anything, but none states
  it outright either (**uncertain**, likely kept).
- **Flora's level**: **uncertain**. No source states it. Indirect: Bolner's high-level run has Jian 24 / Gabryel 23 at
  Elda Canyon and Jian 29 / Gabryel 27 / Flora 25 at the Red Dragon, which fits Flora keeping her level (not a reset to
  1), but the catch-up speed makes this inconclusive.
- **Flora's gear**: **uncertain**. Retro says "You may want to remove anything you want to keep from Flora before
  leaving [Sandra Desert]" (hedged). Ripclaw and darklao give no such warning. Not confirmed either way.
- **Lucia and Rufus**: never return, and **confirmed** leave with whatever they had equipped (Ripclaw, Retro, darklao,
  SaveFAQ, DCblog). The game has no unequip, only swapping (LP U03), so guides tell you to swap in the cheapest items.
- **Rufus' join level**: **confirmed** fixed at 15 in every run reported (Bolner normal and low-level runs, DCblog,
  Hoard). **Uncertain** whether that is a hard-coded value or computed from something.

## Related notes

- **Save glitch (research.md item 7)**: **confirmed** (SaveFAQ) USA-only bug: after Flora leaves, going back to the
  Underground Tunnel and talking to her, or getting a bad fortune from the Lind fortune teller, can grey out Save until
  power off. Not present in the Japanese release. Two deliberate save locks exist: Gronk to Zethos, and the four
  Chamber of Rebirth side rooms. Relevant if the hack makes Flora recruitable from the tunnel.
- **Card collection oddity**: Ripclaw says the Gabryel and Gideon 2 cards cannot be obtained legitimately.
