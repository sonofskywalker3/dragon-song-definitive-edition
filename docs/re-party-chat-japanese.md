# Party chat: the vanilla Y-button lines against the Japanese (research 2026-10-09)

How faithful is each English line of USA script 018 (the Y-button "thinking out loud" hints that are
becoming party chat, docs/re-party-chat.md) to the Japanese original (Lunar Genesis, script 018)? This
covers all 99 distinct English lines, so Jeff can decide which to keep, fix, or rewrite. Pairs, glosses,
and verdicts are below; the method is at the end. Reproduce the pairing with
`uv run python -m dsde.jp_hint_pairs` (src/dsde/jp_hint_pairs.py).

Verdicts: **FAITHFUL** (same meaning and tone; padding and small jokes allowed), **DRIFT** (meaning
kept, but tone, detail, or a speaker's attitude changed in a way a reader would notice), **WRONG**
(meaning changed, a place or name lost, or the hint misleads), **ADDED** (the English says something
the Japanese does not). Real typos are listed on top of the verdict.

| DRIFT | 22 |

### 1.1 The ten most consequential differences

Rows refer to the table in section 3. Suggestions fit the box (30 characters per line, up to 5 lines a
page; `{Place}` and `[item]` as in src/dsde/party_chat_lines.py); every one was checked for width.

1. **Row 96, 0x5204, met Lucia (flag 0xB). WRONG.** Japanese: "If we don't stop by Gad's Express and
   pick up the parcel, the delivery will be late!" English: "We'd better get this package to Gad's
   Express right away". At this point they have no package yet (the next stage, 0x1C, is "take this
   package to Perit"), so the English sends the player the wrong way in the first minutes.
   Suggested: `Lucia: Come on, Jian! We have\nto pick up the [package] at\nGad's Express, or the\ndelivery will be late!`
2. **Row 98, 0x51dc, Lucia left (0xC). WRONG.** Japanese: Jian mutters "Lucia, honestly... where on
   earth did she go?" English: "Oh, Lucia! Where have you been?", spoken to someone who is not there.
   Suggested: `Jian: Ugh, Lucia... Where did\nshe run off to?`
3. **Row 77, 0x54bc, hunting the Armored Boar (0x2C). WRONG.** The Japanese names the place: "The
   Armored Boar has to be hiding somewhere in Roland Forest!" English: "somewhere around this area",
   which is wrong anywhere but the forest (exactly the place-aware problem). Suggested:
   `Jian: The Armored Boar has to\nbe hiding somewhere in\n{Roland Forest}. Let's go find\nit!`
4. **Row 49, 0x5a0c, Gad waiting (0xC9). WRONG.** Japanese: "let's go pick up the package (ひきとりに)!
   Gad is waiting." English: "let's get this package delivered!" Jian's "...Yeah." is silence in
   Japanese. Suggested: `Gabryel: Come on, let's go\npick up that package. Gad's\nwaiting. / Jian: ......`
5. **Row 85, 0x535c, package recovered (0x28). ADDED.** "Thank the Goddess!" / "I didn't see the
   Goddess helping out, but anyway..." is not in the Japanese ("Isn't it great? The parcel came back
   safe!" / "Yeah... let's go back to Perit Village and take it to Gad's Express!"). An early, offhand
   jab at Althena that the story never set up. Suggested: `Lucia: Thank goodness! We got\nthe [package] back safe! / Jian: Yeah... Let's get back\nto {Perit Village} and take\nit to Gad's Express!`
6. **Row 74, 0x5528, to Leephon by ferry (0x34). ADDED, and the speaker tag is misspelled
   "Gabriel".** "You do know your way home, don't you?" replaces "Come on, let's hurry!!". Suggested:
   `Gabryel: If we're headed to\n{Leephon City}, we take the\nferry from {Port Searis}!\nCome on, let's hurry!`
7. **Row 21, 0x5f5c, before the Blue Dragon trial. DRIFT.** In Japanese Jian says "I just..." and
   Flora finishes for him, "...want to save Lucia! ...Right?", and Jian stays silent. The English
   gives Jian "All I want is the strength..." (he wants power) and a closing "...That's right."
   Suggested: `Gabryel: So this is it... Pass\nthe Blue Dragon's trial, and\nyou'll be a Dragonmaster,\nright, Jian? / Jian: That's not decided yet...\nI just... / Flora: ...want to save Lucia.\n...Right? / Jian: ......`
8. **Row 7, 0x62a4, Rufus's sword (0xCE). DRIFT.** Jian's silence becomes "...Yes."; "You did a great
   thing" and "I don't know, it's hard to explain" are added; Flora's shy "Jian, maybe you're kind of
   cool?" becomes the backhanded "way cooler than I expected from you!". Suggested (core pages):
   `Gabryel: Thank you... Rufus\nwas a hero to every beastman.\nIt means a lot to me. / Jian: That's not it! That's not\nit at all... Rufus fought for\nus. He was one of us. / So this isn't about payback.\nAll I know is, our friend's\nsword saved us again. / Flora: Huh... Jian, that was\nkind of cool?`
9. **Row 56, 0x5858, "you two are a couple" (0x1A5). DRIFT.** Gabryel's reason is protective in
   Japanese ("that's not fair to Lucia!") and a jab in English ("what about Lucia, huh?"); "But you
   make a lovely couple" is added; Jian's "...Lucia..." is silence. Suggested: `Gabryel: I mean... think how\nthat would make Lucia feel!\nJian and Lucia are... / Jian: ......`
10. **Row 70, 0x564c, Vile Tribe at the cathedral (0x76). DRIFT.** Gabryel's mock-polite "If you mean
    me, you've got the wrong girl! Sorry, but I wasn't raised that soft. Toodle-oo~" became "What do
    you think this claw is for, bub? Trimming the verge? ... But you can stay behind, if you like." (a
    dig at Jian). Suggested: `Gabryel: If you mean me,\nyou've got the wrong girl!\nSorry, but I wasn't raised\nthat soft. Toodle-oo!`

Not a fault but worth knowing: **row 5, 0x6358 (flags 0x1B8..0x1BB), is USA-only.** The Japanese 018
has no branch on those flags and no such text anywhere in the file: the scene where Jian senses Ignatius
in the Chamber of Rebirth and screams "Luciaaaaaaaaaaa!!!" was added for the USA release.

### 1.2 Patterns in the localization

- **The hints survive.** Of 99 lines, only four mislead (rows 49, 77, 96, 98) and one weakens (row 39:
  "if anything has happened, it'll be at that plinth" for "if there's a trick, it's at the pedestal").
  Destinations and goals are otherwise kept, so most lines can stay as they are.
- **Jokes and jabs are added, mostly at someone's expense.** Gabryel calls Flora dim (row 36, "your
  quiver is a few arrows short"), Flora mocks Kirlis (rows 14 to 16, "old crackpot"), Flora jokes while
  Jian is hurting (row 26, "could depress a clown"), plus "group hug over", "Blah blah blah", "tinder
  head", the honey pun, "funky guy", "bub", "bow girl". The Japanese cast teases, but gently; the
  English cast is snarkier with each other. This is the main DRIFT.
- **Silences are filled in.** Jian's "......" (Japanese …………) becomes a spoken line five times (rows
  4, 7, 21, 49, 56), usually agreeing with whoever just spoke. In Japanese his silence is the point.
- **Errands get reversed.** Picking a package up becomes delivering it (rows 49, 96): the translator
  read あずかる and ひきとる as "deliver".
- **Attitudes flip in small ways.** Flora's sheepish backdown becomes defiance (row 33), her flat "I'll
  never believe it" becomes "until I see it" (row 10), her awe at a "mystical" cave becomes fear (row
  20), Jian's "Damn...!" becomes an upbeat "OK, big trouble ahead!" (row 64).
- **Speakers.** Every line has the same speaker in both releases (the portrait ops and speaker tags
  match). Voice is flattened: Japanese Flora says あたい (a rustic, tomboyish "I") and drawls; Gabryel
  is feminine and a little prim (あたし, ～わ, ごめんあそばせ); Jian is rough (オレ, ～ぜ). The English
  keeps none of these markers.
- **Names the Japanese gives that the English drops** (useful for place-aware chat): Roland Forest (row
  77), Marcella (row 80, "Marcella said it's Insector honey"), Zethos by name (row 48, "Beast King
  Zethos"). The Japanese also says "the town of" / "the village of" for Healriz, Leephon, Noapeace, Perit,
  Rebric, and Lind; the "Great" Cathedral of Althena (アルテナ大せいどう); the Meryod *Undersea* Cave (かいていどうくつ); the
  Blue Dragon *Spring* (イズミ; English "Lake"); the Sungrid *Great* Bridge. Japanese-only names: Gōto Forest
  (ゴートのもり, English Thieves' Woods), Deruri Temple (デルリの神でん, Delrich Temple), Auberge Bay
  (オーベルージュ・ベイ, Port Olbeage). The English names are consistent with the rest of the USA game,
  so keep them.

**"information,right?" (row 93) and "istake" (row 95) are not typos in the game.** Both lines are
exactly 30 characters ("We're looking for information," and "Right, so all we need to do is"), and the
box wraps a full line by itself, so the stored text has no space there. The game shows "information," /
"right?" (build/chat/perit_strip.png, the vanilla line in Perit Village) and "is" / "take". They only
look wrong in raw text dumps; the table below shows the text as the box shows it.

### 1.4 The two trees differ in one place

The USA tree checks 0x1B8..0x1BB (the added scene, row 5) and then 0x1A9 (the final battle line, row
6) before 0xCE (Rufus's sword, rows 3 and 7). The Japanese tree checks 0xCE, then 0x1A9. So in Japanese
the Rufus's sword talk still plays once 0x1A9 is set; in English the final battle line hides it. Every
other branch, flag, and leaf matches one to one.

## 2. Notes for rewriting

- Lines marked FAITHFUL can stay. Most DRIFT lines need one page changed, given in the last column.
- Row 89 (0x52e0, Lucia and the four dragon statues) is DRIFT the other way: the English spells the hint
  out ("suggests some kind of puzzle to me") where Lucia only says "something about them bothers me".
  The English is the better hint; keep it.
- Row 99 (0x51c8, the very first hint): the Japanese also opens with とにかく ("anyway"), so vanilla's
  "Anyway..." is a faithful translation of an equally abrupt original. DSDE's text edit that drops it
  (feat_text.py) is a style choice, not a fix of a translation error.
- Row 66 (0x56ac): "Looks like it fits!" translates のみこめた, which most likely means "now I get it"
  but can also read as "it (the statue) swallowed it". Marked DRIFT with the "I get it" reading.

## 4. Method and uncertainty

- **Text.** USA: build/unpacked/script/018.bin (25,620 bytes). Japanese: build/unpacked_jp/script/018.bin
  (14,848 bytes), decoded with the global font table of `dsde.jp_text` (docs/re-japanese.md), so the
  kanji are the game's own. Same script id, same layout: text from 0x08, code after it (USA 0x5124, JP
  0x2774), the first op jumps to the tree.
- **Alignment by the flag tree, not by order.** `dsde.jp_hint_pairs` walks both first-match trees (ops
  0x13/0x14/0x15 and jumps) and gives every path its full condition: the flags of the branch it takes
  plus the negation of every branch it passes. 90 of the 104 USA paths have a Japanese path with exactly
  the same condition. 13 more pair "loosely": exactly one Japanese path requires the same flags set, and
  the only difference is the negated 0x1B8..0x1BB and 0x1A9 checks (section 1.4). One USA path (0x6358,
  0x1B8..0x1BB) has no counterpart; `jp_text --all` finds no unreferenced Japanese string for it
  either. No Japanese path is left over.
- **Confirmation by the leaves.** Every pair was checked by its body: the portrait ops (face and slot)
  match op for op in all 103 pairs, so do the speakers, the number of message boxes, and the "said once"
  flag ops (0x19) in the same leaves (rows 2, 4, 7, 23, 28, 37, 56, 78). The pairing is
  certain; no line was matched by guesswork.
- **As shown.** The English column re-inserts the line break the box makes after a full 30-character
  line (the stored text has none), so it reads as the game shows it. Checked against the vanilla Perit
  Village screenshot (build/chat/perit_strip.png).
- **Glosses** are mine, literal but readable; sentence-final particles are folded into tone words
  ("huh?", "right?"). Readings of the names in kana follow the USA spellings where they exist.
- **Judgment calls.** The line between FAITHFUL and DRIFT is a judgment: padding and one-word jokes
  that keep the tone are FAITHFUL; anything that changes how a character treats another, or what they
  want, is DRIFT. Uncertain readings: row 66 (のみこめた, "got it" or "swallowed it"), row 49
  (ひきとりに, "to collect"; the story context of 0xC9 was not replayed), and the Gōto Forest reading
  (ゴート could be "Goat"). Nothing was run in the emulator for this file except the existing Perit
  screenshot.
- **Suggested lines** use the party chat format (src/dsde/party_chat_lines.py): `\n` line breaks,
  `{Place}` in blue, `[item]` in the item color, at most 5 lines of 30 characters a page, all checked
  with `dsde.party_chat.shown_width`. They are drafts in Jeff's register for him to rewrite, not
  patches.
